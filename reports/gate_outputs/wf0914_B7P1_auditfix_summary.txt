1. Energy-conservation violation in bridge_power_firstprinciples_c chain
   (RadiationModel::modalEnergyFirstPrinciples / ScoreRenderer.h) -- FIXED.
   Root cause: assembled impulse used F_peak (Hertz energy-conservation
   solve) together with tauC from B4's independently-calibrated
   pianoHammerTauC(), an unrelated quantity. Fix: HammerImpulse::
   hertzImpulseConsistentTauCSeconds(note,v,F_peak) = pi*m*v/F_peak,
   self-consistent by construction with the SAME Hertz solve. Verified
   via tests/physics_models_repro.cpp domain scan (MIDI 36/48/60/72/84/96
   x velocity 20/40/60/77/90/110/120, 42 points): impulse/(2*m*v) worst
   case = 1.000000 (was 2.55-4.32x before the fix; audit's own worked
   example MIDI60/velocity0.8 reproduced at 3.637x via a regression-guard
   test that deliberately calls the old (wrong) pianoHammerTauC() path).
